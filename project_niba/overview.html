PROJECT NIBA - Overview and Proposal written by Dr. [REDACTED] and Dr. Bishop presented on 15 March 1998 DESCRIPTION Project Non-Invasive Brain Alteration, Project NIBA, or simply NIBA, is a scientific program designed with the goal of manipulating the human mind without opening the skull cavity via invasive surgery. Our initial proposal is based around the theory that, by changing the size of certain regions (lobes) in the human brain, the skills associated with or controlled by these regions will be affected. To facilitate this testing, we propose a NIBA Device, or NIBAD, that uses certain frequencies or subatomic particles to allow a beam to pass through the subject's external structures and make contact with grey matter. Initial tests will be conducted on laboratory rats and small mammals before human testing can begin. Assuming the worst-case scenario of little to no concluding evidence in favor of our hypothesis, the program shall run for a minimum of two (2) years. We would like to request the ability to extend the program's life and financial support in the event of a scientific breakthrough. Given the nature of this experimentation, the program will adhere to strict safety and ethics guidelines as outlined at the end of this proposal. Thank you for considering us for funding.
